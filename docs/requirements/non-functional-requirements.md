# Non-Functional Requirements

Quality goals must be measurable. Create every quality requirement as a
separate file under `quality/` using `quality/NFR-template.md`. Use a lowercase
hyphenated title after the identifier, for example
`NFR-PERF-001-response-time.md`.

## Quality requirement index

| Requirement | Title | Status | Priority | Attribute |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `<NFR-ID>` | `<TITLE>` | `<STATUS>` | `<PRIORITY>` | `<ATTRIBUTE>` |

Do not define quality requirements in this index. `Fast`, `secure`, or
`user-friendly` without a measurement method and target is not verifiable.

## Recommended quality review

- Performance and scalability
- Availability, recovery, and data integrity
- Security, privacy, and retention
- Accessibility and usability
- Task success, efficiency, learnability, and error tolerance
- Maintainability, portability, and compatibility
- Observability and operations
